Skills & Requirements

What to look for in an SEO specialist.

Most people searching for SEO help don't know what seo specialist skills actually matter, or what seo specialist requirements separate a real practitioner from a template-seller. Here's the honest checklist I'd give anyone hiring, including how I measure up against it.

Core SEO Specialist Skills

  • Keyword research: reading real search-volume data, not guessing at topics.
  • Technical auditing: finding crawl, speed and indexing issues before they cost rankings.
  • Content strategy: planning pages around what people actually search for, instead of chasing a word count.
  • Data literacy: reads Search Console and Google Analytics directly, rather than relying on someone else's dashboard summary.
  • Clear communication: can explain what changed and why, in plain language.

Requirements to Ask For

The seo specialist requirements below are what I'd expect any client to ask for, mine included.

  • No fake ranking guarantees. Nobody outside Google controls Google's algorithm, so anyone promising #1 is a red flag.
  • A documented process. Audit, strategy, execution, reporting, laid out clearly enough that you could explain it back to someone else.
  • Direct communication. You talk to the person doing the work, not a sales rep relaying updates secondhand.
  • Verifiable reporting. Screenshots and direct access to your own Search Console account, not a PDF summary.
  • Local market knowledge. Understands how people in Nepal actually search.

The short version: the seo specialist skills and seo specialist requirements above aren't a marketing checklist. They're the standard I hold my own SEO work to on every project.
