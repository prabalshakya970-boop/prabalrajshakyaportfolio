FAQ

Common questions about SEO.

What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the work of shaping a website's content, structure and reputation so it shows up higher in Google's organic (unpaid) search results for the terms your customers actually search.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most SEO in Nepal projects start showing measurable movement in Search Console within 6 to 12 weeks, with meaningful ranking gains typically building over 3 to 6 months. It depends on your site's starting point and the difficulty of your target keywords.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Any seo expert who guarantees a #1 ranking isn't being straight with you. Google's algorithm isn't controlled by anyone outside Google. What I can commit to is a verified, data-backed process: real keyword research, technical fixes, and transparent reporting.

What's included in your SEO service in Nepal?

Every engagement follows the same four-step process: audit, strategy, execution and reporting, covering on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO and content strategy. See the Process section above for details.

Do you only work with businesses in Kathmandu?

I'm based in Kathmandu and understand the Nepal search market closely, but I work with clients anywhere. Most SEO work happens remotely, over Search Console, Analytics and screen-share calls.
